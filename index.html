El Fry's
Project Folio

I'm a geology student at Monash Universiy with a keen interest in mapping and structural geo. This site displays a few projects of mine.


Eldee Station Field Mapping

Two weeks on a remote station near Broken Hill, producing a detailed map and cross section of a highly deformed pre-Cambrian terrane.

My mapboard in the field with geological units and structural data shown. This is an unfinished copy, as our final maps can't be returned for 12 months. The rock units mapped were Interbedded Metapsammite & Metapelite (in blue), Retrogressed Amphibolite (in green), Felsic Gneiss (in red), Biotite Gneiss (in purple), and pegmatite (in yellow). Form lines for several generations of foliation are also plotted.

Buchan Field Mapping

A week mapping folded rift sediments in Murrindal and Buchan, focusing on precise mapping and prediction of subsurface geology.

Broken Hill Mapping Project

A 14 day field camp in remote Central NSW, mapping the structure and metamorphism of an intensely deformed pre-Cambrian terrane. Production of a detailed geological map and cross section.

Omeo Isograds & Volcanics

- Mapping of metamorphic mineral isograds betweeen Omeo and Switft's Creek, through a historic gold mining region.
- Investigating the relationship between intrusive syenite and volcaniclastic deposits at Day Hill.

Buchan Field Mapping Camp

A week-long project developing skills in field geology and mapping. Resulting in the production of both local and regional-scale geological maps and cross sections.
